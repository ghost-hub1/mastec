Installing EV infrastructure has never been easier!

Transform your project with our turnkey solution that ensures seamless integration from initial scoping to post-construction, safeguarding your operations to run smoothly and efficiently.

Our comprehensive suite of EV services includes preliminary review and scoping, site acquisition, material management, construction, and modified maintenance and dispatch services.

MasTec, with its national footprint, brings decades of civil construction expertise and a track record of thousands of successful site completions. We distinguish ourselves in the nationwide implementation of expansive and intricate deployment projects through the utilization of our highly skilled in-house civil construction crews and trained, licensed electricians.

Our team stands as our greatest asset, bringing a wealth of experience across all facets of EV charger installation, from fundamental Level 1 units to advanced Level 3 chargers. We ensure expert service at every stage.


Site Design:

MasTec works with major charging manufacturers to provide ROW and/or land acquisition services using the latest AI and data analytics. We can provide station layout designs, both physical and electrical, and prepare engineering drawings for review, permitting, and construction. As part of our site design services, MasTec can manage all permit applications, submissions, and tracking.


Installation:

For new installations and charger replacements, MasTec provides coordination between utility teams, customer stakeholders, and installers to prepare each site. During construction and installation, our installers ensure all safety regulations are met.


Operations & Maintenance:

MasTec is a trusted EV Charger maintenance and operations partner. When replacement equipment is needed to keep your chargers functioning properly, our maintenance specialists make sure they are procured, stored securely, and made available.
