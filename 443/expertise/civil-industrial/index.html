Civil and industrial construction services.

MasTec provides turnkey solutions for a variety complex civil infrastructure construction projects. We also offer specialized foundation construction with established divisions specializing in heavy construction, road and bridge construction, earth moving, land development and subdivision preparation, including underground utilities rough-in.

MasTec provides a full range of industrial construction support to a variety of industries from Agricultural Processing facilities to Biofuels and Manufacturing. Our wide range of expert construction services provide our clients with safe and cost-effective projects.

While speed and accuracy are our hallmarks, safety remains our top priority. Formal safety audits are performed as part of every project. All supervisory staff receives OSHA certification and continuous training to keep current with upgrades in equipment and changes to rules and regulations.

Wanzek Construction

Headquartered in Fargo, North Dakota, Wanzek was founded on heavy/civil and concrete construction projects. By 2000, Wanzek established a large crane service division and a resume of projects for industrial and renewable clients across the Midwest. In 2008, MasTec, Inc. acquired Wanzek, providing continued growth and synergy in Wanzek’s future. Wanzek is a platform company within the MasTec Clean Energy & Infrastructure Group but retains its name, culture and operating structure. Today we are providing the highest quality EPC services for renewable energy. Our vision inspires us every day and in every aspect of our company and services. To us, it means being the best by making the commitment to details that deliver excellence in every job, big and small. To our customers, it means having a responsive, dependable and capable general contractor and service provider with the experience and project execution history that creates a lasting relationship.

White Construction

White Construction began as a small family-owned trucking and excavating company in the 1940s. Since our founding, the organization has grown tremendously, become a part of IEA and even reached publicly-traded status—yet the values that the White family instilled in the company remain core to our culture today. White Construction is a subsidiary of Infrastructure and Energy Alternatives, Inc. (IEA), a leading infrastructure construction company with specialized energy and heavy civil expertise. In 2022, IEA and its affiliated companies were acquired by MasTec and are now part of the organization's Clean Energy and Infrastructure segment. At White Construction, we're able to leverage these companies' robust resources, diverse capabilities and national reach to deliver even greater value to our customers.

MasTec Canada

MasTec Canada Inc. is a diversified infrastructure construction company that operates throughout Canada with a focus on the four western provinces of British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba. MasTec Canada offers a broad scope of services and is managed by a Canadian team that has been working together for the past 12 years. Our services include pipeline construction ranging from 2"-36"; facility construction focusing on wellsites, gas plants, compressor and pump stations; heavy civil construction, earthworks and concrete; temporary and permanent buildings construction. Primarily serving the role of General Contractor, MasTec Canada Inc. has the equipment, experience, personnel and systems to execute complex energy and industrial construction programs safely and efficiently.

Casey Industrial

Casey Industrial has self-performed projects in multiple markets across the Continental USA since 1947 with completed projects in 46 states. Clients appreciate our willingness to provide single-point accountability for delivering the complete scope in a safe, timely, and cost-effective manner. Project development, pre-construction planning, project management, outage support, and fabrication are just a few of the added-value services we provide. Project communications and execution are optimized thanks to the multi-craft, in-house synergies of foundation work, steel erection, millwright, process piping, and electrical power & controls. Major focus markets include power generation, minerals processing, building materials manufacturing, and chemicals, executing as an EPC or construction only contractor. Our people have the mastery to manage the right people, equipment, and materials in the right place at the right time. With every project, we commit to the client’s schedule and budget, providing the highest levels of quality, safety, and value.

IEA Constructors

Infrastructure and Energy Alternatives, Inc. (IEA) is a leading infrastructure construction company with specialized energy and heavy civil expertise. Headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana, with operations throughout the country, IEA’s service offering spans the entire construction process. The company provides a full spectrum of delivery models including full engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC), turnkey, design-build, balance of plant (BOP), and subcontracting services. IEA is one of three Tier 1 wind energy contractors in the United States, and has completed more than 265 wind and solar projects across 36 states. In the heavy civil space, IEA offers a number of specialty services including rail work, environmental management, industrial maintenance, and other site development for public and private projects.
