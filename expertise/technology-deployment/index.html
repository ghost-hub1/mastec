People. Process. Infrastructure. Results.

MasTec Advanced Technologies is the leader in last mile fulfillment services for the deployment and installation of technology and IoT devices. We safely deliver, install and support an array of consumer and commercial products, creating a high-quality customer experience in every interaction. We are the “clean boot, white glove” service provider, with more than 3,000 full-time SmartConnect service professionals and technicians nationwide. We connect people with the products and technology they want and need from TV, entertainment and security to connected homes, facility upgrades and smart cities. We provide the planning, staffing, training, resource deployment, inventory management, call center support and analytics required for a comprehensive workforce solution. Our controls deliver the depth, flexibility, technology and efficiency needed to meet any business objective. With over 25 years of expertise and one of the largest fleets of service vehicles in the country, you can be confident in us to deliver success for your business.

    Video

    Video


    MasTec is one of the largest last mile installation and fulfillment service companies in the country. We have a network of SmartConnect service professionals who deliver, install and support video delivery solutions (satellite, cable, OTT) to both residential and commercial customers. Our dedicated management and training teams ensure the right resources in the right place at the right time to ensure quality installations and high customer satisfaction.

    Internet of Things (IoT)

    Internet of Things (IoT)


    As the Internet of Things (IoT) become a larger and larger part of our daily life, MasTec is on the leading edge to ensure you have the ability to connect customers, devices, businesses and communities to the integrated entertainment, communications and intelligent systems that matter most to them.

    Smart Home Technologies

    Smart Home Technologies


    We are partnering with the most trusted brands to deliver Smart Home products and solutions directly to a customer’s home or business creating an “always-on, always accessible” connected environment. We offer a suite of commercial services including structured wiring, access control, Wi-Fi, network design and installation.

    Consumer Electronics

    Consumer Electronics


    We work with some of the largest CE manufacturers in the world, supporting sales and understanding that customer education is the key to ensuring the best possible customer experience. We help CE companies and CE retailers extend the customer experience into the home with full solutions and complete systems. For example, we don’t just deliver HDTVs, we can mount the TV, provision service, provide surge protection, sound bars and high quality cables to enhance the user experience. Retail is changing and our presence in the home provides brands a guaranteed personal touch to deliver on their brand promise.

    Telecommunications

    Telecommunications


    Our business tracks the latest product introductions in the Telecommunication sectors to be able to quickly mobilize the people and skills our clients need to distribute products and support their customers. MasTec ensures an understanding and the best practices to sell, install and provision products and has maintained successive generations of service delivery for some of the nation’s largest wireless, broadband and digital carriers.

    Security

    Security


    MasTec has developed the largest geographic service fulfillment footprint for installation, support and upgrades of residential and commercial security products providing command and control, cameras, surveillance and monitored service offerings from the leading security providers in the nation.

    Smart Cities

    Smart Cities


    MasTec partners with municipalities to support their smart city transformations. Specializing in product deployment, installation, provisioning and comprehensive program management, we deliver expertise in LED lighting retrofits, energy monitoring and analysis, lighting and traffic controls, water management and more as a trusted service provider, project management resource and installation arm for your smart city and industrial IoT partner ecosystem.
