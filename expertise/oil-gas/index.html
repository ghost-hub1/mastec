Oil and natural gas pipeline and facility construction.

MasTec provides a full range of oil and natural gas construction services including oil pipelines, natural gas pipelines, compressor and pump stations, and gas and oil processing facilities. Our experienced engineers and construction crews stay at the forefront of software, equipment and training developments to ensure the most effective design and construction of processing, oil and natural gas pipeline systems. Our drive for innovation, quality and safety allows us to complete any project on time and on budget, with minimal impact to landowners, total environmental compliance and maximum overall efficiency.

  • COMPRESSOR / PUMPING STATIONS
  • ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE

Services Provided by:

MasTec Utility Services

MasTec Utility Services is a nationwide network of professionals providing critical infrastructure construction and engineering services for communications, power delivery and gas customers, 24/7. Utility Services specializes in wireline construction for fiber and copper communications networks; overhead and underground electric distribution for power delivery systems; and gas distribution construction for gas systems. MasTec Utility Services is committed to safely providing infrastructure that delivers.

MasTec Canada

MasTec Canada Inc. is a diversified infrastructure construction company that operates throughout Canada with a focus on the four western provinces of British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba. MasTec Canada offers a broad scope of services and is managed by a Canadian team that has been working together for the past 12 years. Our services include pipeline construction ranging from 2"-36"; facility construction focusing on wellsites, gas plants, compressor and pump stations; heavy civil construction, earthworks and concrete; temporary and permanent buildings construction. Primarily serving the role of General Contractor, MasTec Canada Inc. has the equipment, experience, personnel and systems to execute complex energy and industrial construction programs safely and efficiently.

Wanzek Construction

Headquartered in Fargo, North Dakota, Wanzek was founded on heavy/civil and concrete construction projects. By 2000, Wanzek established a large crane service division and a resume of projects for industrial and renewable clients across the Midwest. In 2008, MasTec, Inc. acquired Wanzek, providing continued growth and synergy in Wanzek’s future. Wanzek is a platform company within the MasTec Clean Energy & Infrastructure Group but retains its name, culture and operating structure. Today we are providing the highest quality EPC services for renewable energy. Our vision inspires us every day and in every aspect of our company and services. To us, it means being the best by making the commitment to details that deliver excellence in every job, big and small. To our customers, it means having a responsive, dependable and capable general contractor and service provider with the experience and project execution history that creates a lasting relationship.

Hanging H

Hanging H is a tight-knit group of professionals that emphasizes low turnover, teamwork and doing what it takes to get the work done. We are committed to providing the resources required to educate, train and mentor our craftsmen. Our success is the direct result of the investment in our Health, Safety and Environmental programs.
