Electric transmission and distribution services.

As one of the nation's leading electric transmission line construction companies, MasTec engineers and constructs efficient and reliable electrical transmission, distribution and substation systems. Our extensive workforce includes both union and nonunion teams which, combined with our specialized equipment fleet, allows  us to effectively meet our client's needs for power delivery, emergency restoration, and on-going maintenance. We've been involved in some of the most challenging and recognizable electric projects across the U.S.

From the electric plant, to the end users, our experienced managers and crews consistently deliver transmission, substation, and distribution services with an unfailing commitment to safety, emergency restoration, and environmental concerns.

  • SUBSTATIONS AND SWITCHYARDS
  • DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS
  • EMERGENCY RESTORATION
  • SMART GRID
  • MAINTENANCE

Services Provided by:

Henkels & McCoy (H&M)

Henkels & McCoy (H&M) is an industry-leading utility construction firm providing critical infrastructure design, construction, and maintenance services for the power, oil & gas pipeline, gas distribution, and communication markets throughout North America. H&M has been recognized with an E.I. DuPont Safety Excellence Award and is a founding member of the ET&D Partnership, a collaborative industry partnership working to reduce fatalities, injuries, and illnesses in the electric line construction industry. H&M builds every project and relationship on a foundation of safety, integrity, and trust.

3 Phase Line Construction

3Phase Line's expertise lies in substations and switchyards, transmission services, distribution and telecommunications; all constructed while maintaining the strictest environmental and safety compliances. Their highly skilled professionals, advanced equipment, and unprecedented work ethic make them our partner in excellence for electrical contracting.

Alvah Contractors

Alvah Group acts as the General Contractor and Program Manager for large scale utility projects and Master Service Agreements handling all aspects necessary to complete projects in a timely and safe fashion; and provide continuous Project time line and cost monitoring to allow our customers to have complete visibility into their spend. Alvah Group, Inc. is a certified MBE in the State of California. Alvah Group relies on the expertise of its affiliated companies, Alvah Contracting, LLC, Utility Construction Services, Inc., Utility Traffic Control Services, Inc. and Utility Traffic & Restoration, Inc. to provide seamless operations to its customer. These entities are uniquely focused on all aspects of construction for the Electric Power Industry. These services include Overhead line construction and Replacement; Underground line construction and maintenance; Duct work and manhole installation and maintenance, site restoration and all aspects of traffic control. The operating entities specialize in working in dense urban areas/city centers but are equally at home in suburban/rural environments.

Bianchi Electric

Bianchi Electric is an electrical utility contractor with complete transmission, substation and underground construction capabilities. Headquartered in Dallas/Fort Worth, Texas, the company specializes in utility installation, supporting ET&D projects from start to finish and tackling customers’ most complex and technical service challenges. Bianchi’s high-voltage capabilities are diverse, and include engineering and construction management, underground, transmission and substation construction and maintenance.

MasTec Utility Services

MasTec Utility Services is a nationwide network of professionals providing critical infrastructure construction and engineering services for communications, power delivery and gas customers, 24/7. Utility Services specializes in wireline construction for fiber and copper communications networks; overhead and underground electric distribution for power delivery systems; and gas distribution construction for gas systems. MasTec Utility Services is committed to safely providing infrastructure that delivers.

Energy Erectors, Inc.

Our broad range of expertise includes GIS, HVDV, Iso-phase bus installations, transmission spur lines, and VAR compensators. We provide these services to the electric power industry; and to industrial, heavy commercial, and water / wastewater industries.

EC Source Services

HMI Technical Solutions (HMITS)
